About

Terranthro is a geospatial studio in Portland, Oregon, founded by Hayden Smith.

The studio

More and more of us live disconnected from the land around us. We drive past hillsides, vineyards and river valleys every day without knowing why they look the way they do, or what they hold.

Terranthro exists to close that gap. We're a mapmaking studio for understanding what the Earth holds: the climate, terrain and soil beneath a place, and the people who work it. Our cartography takes whatever form tells that story best, from interactive maps to pieces you can hang on a wall, and all of it is built on real data about the land.

So far most of that work has been in wine, where the ground really does show up in the glass. Slope, aspect, elevation, soil and the warmth of a growing season all leave their mark, and a good map lets you see them side by side.

The name

Terra · earth + Anthropos · human

The land and the people who live on it. Terranthro maps sit where the two meet: the physical ground on one side, and on the other everything people have built, farmed and drawn across it.

Work with us

If it happens on the ground, we can map it. Bring us a region, a dataset or a question you can't answer yet. One vineyard or an entire state, clean data or none at all, we'll find what's there and build the map that shows it.

We're also glad to hear from people who want to build this kind of work with us. Send a résumé and a note about what draws you to it.

Either way, email hayden@terranthro.com.
